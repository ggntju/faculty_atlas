Read the full FAQ ↗
Where does the information come from?

Faculty Atlas gathers information from official public university pages. Records include source context and a last-checked date when available.

Which institutions are covered?

The service focuses on U.S. R1 and R2 research universities under the 2025 Carnegie Classification. Visit the coverage explorer for the current dataset.

Can I try it before buying credits?

Yes. Explore the sample builder, then sign in to reveal sample email addresses at no credit cost. You can also preview context before unlocking a paid list.

How should contact lists be used?

Access is licensed for approved professional use. Faculty Atlas does not send email campaigns. Resale and redistribution are prohibited, and faculty opt-outs are permanently suppressed from future generated lists. Read the terms of service for details.
